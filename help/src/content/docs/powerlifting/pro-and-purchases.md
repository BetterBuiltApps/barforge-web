Barforge Powerlifting is free to use for logging your training, building your own workouts, tracking your records and the free 4-week foundation block. **Pro** is an optional subscription that adds the rest of the built-in programs. See [Built-in programs](/help/powerlifting/programs/).

## Subscribe on iPhone

Pro is sold through the App Store, monthly or annually. The price is shown in the app before you buy.

1. Open a Pro feature, or the Pro screen in the app.
2. Choose monthly or annual and confirm with your Apple ID.

To change or cancel, open the **Settings** app on your iPhone, tap your name, then **Subscriptions**.

:::note[Android]
On Android, Pro is one subscription sold through Google Play, and the app shows the price Google Play gives it. To change or cancel, open the **Play Store**, tap your profile picture, then **Payments and subscriptions**, then **Subscriptions**.
:::

## Separate from Barforge Weightlifting

Barforge Powerlifting has its own purchases. A Pro subscription to Barforge Weightlifting does not unlock Pro in Barforge Powerlifting, and Pro in Barforge Powerlifting does not unlock Barforge Weightlifting. If you want Pro in both apps, subscribe in each.

## Payments and privacy

Apple or Google handles the payment. The app only asks the store whether your account has an active subscription, and never sees your card details.
